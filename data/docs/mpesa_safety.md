# M-Pesa Safety Tips

Workshop sample data. Always confirm details with official Safaricom channels.

## Protect your PIN

Never share your M-Pesa PIN with anyone, including people who claim to be Safaricom staff. Safaricom will never ask for your PIN over the phone or by SMS. Change your PIN if you think someone has seen it.

## Spotting fraud messages

Genuine M-Pesa confirmation messages come from the sender name "MPESA", not from a normal phone number. If someone sends you a message saying they sent money "by mistake", check your actual M-Pesa balance before sending anything back. Forward suspicious fraud messages to 333.

## Sent money to the wrong number?

Act quickly. Request a reversal through official Safaricom channels and keep the transaction code from the confirmation message. Do not deal with strangers who call you offering to "help" with a reversal.
