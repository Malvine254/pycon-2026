# Maize Farming Basics in Kenya

Workshop sample data. For advice specific to your county, talk to your local agricultural extension officer.

## When to plant

In many maize-growing regions, planting is timed with the onset of the long rains (around March to April). Planting early, as soon as the rains are reliable, usually gives better yields than late planting.

## Spacing and seed

A common spacing is 75 cm between rows and about 25 to 30 cm between plants, with one seed per hole. Use certified seed suited to your altitude and rainfall zone.

## Fertiliser

Apply a planting fertiliser such as DAP in the planting hole and mix it with soil so the seed does not touch it directly. Top-dress with a nitrogen fertiliser such as CAN when the maize is about knee high.

## Pests: fall armyworm

Scout fields weekly, especially in the first six weeks. Look for ragged holes in the leaves and sawdust-like droppings in the funnel of the plant. Early detection makes control much easier.
